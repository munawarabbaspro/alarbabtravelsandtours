### Titles & meta descriptions of top-ranking sites (12 searches, ~95 results, Oct 2026)
Meta descriptions are not a Google ranking factor; they only affect clicks. Findings:
- Google rewrote most snippets: it showed phone numbers, office hours, FAQ answers or document lists from the page instead of the meta description.
- Local results ("visa consultant in islamabad") show a phone number in the snippet (Vision, Wanderer, Gold Touch, Euroimmi, Rehman).
- Trust phrases: "Pakistan's No. 1", "two decades", "12 years in the US Embassy visa section", "Free assessment / free appointment".
- Country pages: titles with year ("…from Pakistan 2026"), "Requirements", "Complete guide"; snippets show a document list, processing days or a dated FAQ ("05-May-2026 — …").
- Prices appear in many country/Umrah/honeymoon snippets (we deliberately don't).
- Some titles use emoji flags (🇬🇧 🇨🇦 🕋) to stand out.
Applied: country descriptions now "X visa from Pakistan 2026: N-document checklist, how to apply & refusal reasons. Free file check, Blue Area. WhatsApp 0315-2323811"; home and local pages add "since 2009" and the number.

## Biggest problem found: three Al Arbab websites compete with each other
alarbabtravel.com, alarbabtravels.com and alarbabtravel.pk all show the same phone numbers and office. Google splits ranking signals between them and may treat the content as duplicate.
**Fix:** choose ONE main domain, put the new site on it, and 301-redirect every page of the other two domains to the matching page on the main domain. Keep only one website link on the Google Business Profile.
